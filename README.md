I'm a high school junior planning to attend Missouri S&T after graduation and dual major in aerospace and mechanical engineering. I like designing things, figuring out how they work, and getting them built. My projects range from RC aircraft and robot parts to a gear calculator, a custom PC, and a workshop design.

I'm also considering a business minor or another minor that supports my engineering career and interest in useful research and development.

I take PLTW pre-engineering at Cape CTC. On FTC Team 3665, the LEOPOLD Gear Heads, I handle programming, lead CAD design, and produce custom parts. Outside engineering, I spend time on photography, National Beta Club projects, and community service.
